🔑 What is a BIP39 Seed?

A BIP39 seed is a human-readable way to represent the random entropy that secures your cryptocurrency wallet. It consists of 12-24 words chosen from a standardized list of 2048 words.

How it works:

  1. Random entropy is generated (typically 128-256 bits)
  2. A checksum is added to detect errors
  3. The combined bits are split into groups that map to words from the BIP39 wordlist
  4. The resulting phrase can recreate your wallet's private keys

Example: "abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon abandon about"
